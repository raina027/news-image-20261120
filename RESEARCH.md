## Corrections to the top-5 entries' claims (found by live verification)

- **Kyiv is NOT "~30 ballistic missiles, 7 waves, Dutch/Austrian embassies hit".**
  The top entries conflate two attacks. The embassy damage (Dutch, Austrian,
  Swedish) was a **19 Nov** drone attack (98 drones/missiles, 1 killed, 5 injured).
  The **20 Nov** overnight attack is a separate, bigger one: 13 ballistic missiles,
  1 Oreshnik, 22 cruise missiles, 242 drones; 4 killed in Kyiv; the **Qatar**
  embassy damaged; 20 residential buildings damaged; 3 Dnipro bridges damaged
  (Zelenskyy; Kyiv Independent). This entry uses the 20 Nov figures only.
- **Cyclone Akvan winds:** the 90-100 km/h gusting 110 figure was the FORECAST.
  Actual landfall winds were 70-80 km/h gusting 90 (IMD). This entry uses the
  actual figure.
- **US Senate Iran war-powers vote (51-48) was Thursday 19 Nov**, 2:42 p.m. ET
  (Reuters, USA Today). It is NOT a 20 Nov story. It goes in DATE CHECK only.
- **GTA VI sales:** the $2.36bn / 24.58m copies figure is a Sensor Tower ESTIMATE
  (updated Thu evening 19 Nov, reported Fri 20 Nov). Take-Two has released NO
  official numbers. This entry labels it CLAIM.
- **Daraxonrasib 60.7%** is from a small open-label Phase 1/2 trial (61 patients,
  no control arm), presented Friday 20 Nov at ENA Barcelona. This entry says so.
- **Bukele** registered his candidacy on Thu 19 Nov (deadline day), reported
  widely on Fri 20 Nov. Kept as a Friday-reported story, worded as a filing.
- **Anthropic:** the IPO priced at $83 and debuted on Thu 19 Nov (US); the first
  CLOSE ($97.17, +17%) was reported Friday in Asian time zones. Kept as a Friday
  story, worded as the first close, with the $83 pricing in DATE CHECK.
- **GTA VI launch** itself was midnight NZ Thu 19 Nov; the sales estimate was
  reported Fri 20 Nov. Same treatment as Anthropic.
